Our mission

10%
of gross revenue goes to charitable causes — by design, before costs

Titan-Gen is a one-person, part-time studio, and giving back is built into how it runs — not something left for “once we’re profitable.” Ten percent of gross revenue from every app is committed to good causes. Every app supports something specific.

Where it goes

Each app is tied to a cause, so support for the work is also support for something bigger.

Education

Lottery Calculator

Primarily the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation

A share of the ad revenue this app earns is directed toward educational causes and access to learning.

Trees & environment

Coffee support (Lottery Calculator)

Tree planting via the Arbor Day Foundation

If you buy the developer a coffee, the tip helps keep the app going — and at the bigger milestones, the developer plants a tree for it.

Clean water · where it started

Urinal Etiquette (retired)

Supported Water.org

The first app I ever shipped — my “hello world” for Android. It’s no longer on the store, but it funded clean drinking water for communities that needed it.

The coffee jar & the coffee “farm”

Future Feature: Lottery Calculator lets you buy the developer a coffee as a thank-you. Buy more than one and the jar grows — and once it reaches the tree tiers, the developer plants a real tree with a tree-planting foundation for each one.

→ → → →
The coffee will be a tip that supports the developer (and removes ads). Planting the tree is something the developer chooses to do with that support — your purchase isn’t a charitable donation, and 100% of it is handled through normal app billing.

Kept honest

This is a small, part-time effort, so there’s no glossy annual report — just a standing commitment and a short list of the causes above. As the apps grow, so does what they can give.
